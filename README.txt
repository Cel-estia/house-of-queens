THE HOUSE OF QUEENS WEBSITE
============================

Open index.html in a browser to preview the website.

Concept:
The House of Queens is positioned as a modern luxury fashion/lifestyle house.
The first version focuses on brand storytelling rather than trying to sell dozens
of products immediately.

Suggested next steps:
1. Replace the placeholder collection cards with real products.
2. Create real product photography and campaign images.
3. Add a proper shop/catalogue and payment system once products are ready.
4. Create Instagram/TikTok accounts with the same visual identity.
5. Publish 1-2 Queen's Journal stories per month.
6. Add a real email/newsletter service later.
7. Register the domain and social handles after checking availability.
